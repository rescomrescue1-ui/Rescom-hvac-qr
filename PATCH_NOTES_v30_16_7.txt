RES-COM HVAC QR — VERSION 30.16.7
Released: Aug 20, 2026 3:04 PM

SIGNED-IN EQUIPMENT ACCESS
- A signed-in Res-Com employee no longer has to enter the 4-digit Company PIN to open equipment.
- Opening/searching/scanning a QR goes directly to the equipment record after employee sign-in.
- The 4-digit Company PIN remains available for employee account creation and personal-PIN reset authorization.
- Existing QR URLs, Unit IDs, Airtable equipment/service history, and the fixed 4-label print sheet are unchanged.
