RES-COM HVAC QR — v30.10
Released: Aug 19, 2026 5:40 PM

RELIABLE TEMPORARY PASSWORDS
- Staff Sign-In QR now uses separate Airtable fields: Staff QR Hash and Staff QR Expires.
- Generating a Staff QR no longer replaces or invalidates an Admin-generated temporary password.
- Admin-generated RC temp codes use Reset Token Hash / Reset Expires as a dedicated one-time login path.
- Generating a temp code no longer overwrites the employee's permanent PBKDF2 password.
- The Admin screen reads the temp token back from Airtable and verifies the exact displayed RC code before showing VERIFIED AGAINST AIRTABLE.
- On successful temp-code sign-in, Res-Com promotes that exact code to the normal employee password and consumes the temp token.
- Staff QR and temporary password can now be active at the same time.
- Staff QR CLOSE is marked as a bound button to eliminate the false CLOSE bug report.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing equipment QR links remain unchanged.
- Existing employee accounts, service history, self-service password reset, Guest Mode, and Company PIN remain compatible.
