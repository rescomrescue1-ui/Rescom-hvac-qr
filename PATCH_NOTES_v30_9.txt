RES-COM HVAC QR — VERSION 30.9
Released: Aug 19, 2026 5:31 PM

SELF-SERVICE PASSWORD RECOVERY
- Added FORGOT / RESET PASSWORD on employee Sign In.
- Employee enters the email used for the existing Res-Com account.
- Res-Com confirms the account, then requires the current 4-digit Company PIN.
- Employee enters and confirms a new password.
- A new random salt + PBKDF2 SHA-256 password hash is written to Airtable.
- The app re-reads and verifies the new password before considering the reset complete.
- Old temporary reset-code fields are cleared after the reset.
- Successful reset signs the employee in and starts the protected session window.

MOBILE / BUTTON RECOVERY
- Password reset uses a dedicated full-screen, touch-scrollable overlay.
- Added extra keyboard-safe bottom space for iPhone and Android.
- Strengthened Create Employee Account scrolling the same way.
- Sign In, Forgot Password, Continue, Reset, Back, Guest, and Create Account controls are explicitly bound before use.

SECURITY / COMPATIBILITY
- Knowing an employee email by itself is not enough to reset the password; the current Company PIN is required.
- No readable password is stored in Airtable.
- Existing equipment QR URLs, Staff QR sign-in, equipment IDs, service history, Team accounts, and prior Patch Notes remain compatible.
