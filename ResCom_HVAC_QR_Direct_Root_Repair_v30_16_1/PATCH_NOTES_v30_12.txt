Res-Com HVAC QR v30.12 — PIN Save Verification Fix
Released: Aug 19, 2026 6:00 PM

FIXED
- Fixed false “PIN did not verify after saving” after Airtable had already accepted the new personal PIN.
- v30.11 wrote the PIN successfully, then immediately re-listed all employee accounts; a briefly stale follow-up response could make verification look like it failed.
- v30.12 verifies the exact PIN salt/hash from Airtable's PATCH save response first.
- Added multiple short read-back retries if the save response cannot be used.
- A confirmed successful PIN write is retained locally instead of throwing a false lockout error.

UNCHANGED
- Employee login: email + 6-digit personal PIN.
- Company authorization: 4-digit Company PIN for account creation / PIN reset only.
- Staff Sign-In QR remains a separate one-use Admin option.
- Permanent equipment QR URLs, equipment IDs, service history, and GitHub Pages path remain unchanged.
