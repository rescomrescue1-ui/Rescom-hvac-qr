RES-COM SAFE RELEASE WORKFLOW — FUTURE UPDATES

1. Production always remains at the permanent repository root.
2. A new candidate is tested separately and must never change equipment QR destinations.
3. Candidate builds must not register a production service-worker scope or become the installed PWA start URL.
4. Kai or Paul tests the candidate as Developer.
5. Only after approval is the approved production ZIP uploaded to the repository root.
6. Active field sessions are not automatically reloaded; the new build takes effect after the app is safely closed/reopened.
7. Keep a copy of the previous production ZIP for rollback.
