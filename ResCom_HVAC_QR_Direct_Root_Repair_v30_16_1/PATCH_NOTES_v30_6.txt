RES-COM HVAC QR — VERSION 30.6
Released: Aug 19, 2026 4:50 PM

EMPLOYEE LOGIN / TEMP PASSWORD
- Admin-generated temporary passwords now have two verification paths:
  1. normal salted PBKDF2 password hash
  2. dedicated 24-hour one-time reset-token hash
- New temporary-password format: RC-XXXX-XXXX.
- Hyphens, spaces, and letter case are normalized at sign-in.
- When the one-time reset token succeeds, Res-Com promotes that exact code into the normal password hash and consumes the reset token.
- Reset tokens expire after 24 hours.

TEAM / ACCOUNT DELETE
- Replaced Disable/Enable with permanent DELETE.
- Deleting an employee removes the App Account from Airtable.
- Existing equipment and service-history records are not deleted.
- Current signed-in Admin cannot delete their own account.
- The final Admin cannot be deleted.

STALE ACCOUNT CLEANUP
- If an App Account is manually deleted from Airtable, phones holding that deleted employee in local storage automatically sign it out.
- This removes stale names such as a deleted technician still appearing in the app.

IPHONE CREATE ACCOUNT
- Fixed full-screen account panel scrolling.
- The 4-digit Company PIN and CREATE TECH ACCOUNT button remain reachable with the iPhone keyboard open.
- Opening CREATE EMPLOYEE ACCOUNT scrolls the form into view.

BRANDING
- Top Res-Com header now uses the higher-resolution 512px app icon.
- Forced full opacity and increased contrast/saturation for a clearer logo.

COMPATIBILITY
- Existing QR links, Unit IDs, equipment records, service history, Guest mode, and prior patch notes remain compatible.
