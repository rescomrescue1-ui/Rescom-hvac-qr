Res-Com HVAC QR v30.11 — Personal PIN Sign-In
Released: Aug 19, 2026 5:49 PM

• Replaced the active employee password sign-in screen with email + a 6-digit personal PIN.
• Existing employees can tap SET / RESET PIN, enter the email already on their account, verify the current 4-digit Company PIN, and choose their own PIN.
• New self-created employee accounts choose a 6-digit personal PIN during signup.
• Admin Add Employee now creates only the employee record — it no longer generates a temporary password.
• Admin-created employees choose their own PIN from the Sign In screen.
• Signed-in employees have CHOOSE / CHANGE MY PIN in Team.
• Staff Sign-In QR remains available as a separate one-use Admin sign-in method.
• Create Account and Set / Reset PIN use full-screen keyboard-safe touch scrolling with extra bottom space on iPhone and Android.
• Added dedicated Airtable Personal PIN Salt, Personal PIN Hash, and Personal PIN Updated fields. The readable PIN is never stored.
• Old password fields remain only for backward data compatibility and are not used by the v30.11 sign-in UI.
• Existing equipment QR links, service history, customer data, and the permanent GitHub Pages path are unchanged.
