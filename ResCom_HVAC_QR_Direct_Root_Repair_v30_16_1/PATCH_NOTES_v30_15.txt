RES-COM HVAC QR — VERSION 30.15
Released: Aug 20, 2026 12:25 PM

DIAGNOSTICS / SIGN-IN
- Diagnostics now distinguish Bug, Warning, User Mistake, Navigation, Action, Success, and System events.
- Screen navigation, button actions, and field selections are recorded without logging PINs, passwords, cloud tokens, or typed text values.
- Wrong PINs and similar sign-in mistakes are recorded as User Mistake instead of software bugs.
- Sign-in failures show a diagnostic/error code and the Sign In screen includes recent sign-in issues.
- Admin receives a repeated-user-mistake warning when the same mistake becomes common across employees.
- Generic Safari "Script error." without usable source details is classified as a Warning instead of a confirmed app failure.

FORGOT PIN / EMAIL RESET CODE
- Added FORGOT PIN? EMAIL RESET CODE.
- The existing personal PIN is never recoverable because Airtable stores only its salt/hash.
- Res-Com creates a 15-minute, one-use six-digit reset code, stores the verification hash/expiry on the employee account, and creates a PIN Reset Requests record for email delivery.
- After the employee enters the emailed reset code, they choose a new six-digit personal PIN and sign in.
- Actual email delivery requires the one-time Airtable Automation described in AIRTABLE_PIN_RESET_EMAIL_SETUP.txt.

EQUIPMENT + STAFF QR
- Permanent equipment QR URL, QR setup status, QR scan count, latest scan time, and scanner email are tracked in Airtable.
- Existing equipment QR records were backfilled in Airtable without changing any printed QR destination.
- Newly generated blank QR labels are registered in Airtable immediately when online; offline registration queues for later cloud sync.
- Staff Sign-In QR is checked before normal startup even if another employee is already signed in.
- Staff QR generated/used timestamps and counts are saved to Airtable. The readable Staff QR secret is NOT stored.

OFFLINE PHOTOS
- Equipment and service photos that cannot upload are compressed and stored in a durable IndexedDB photo queue.
- Queued photos retry automatically when the device returns online and the matching Airtable record exists.
- Equipment setup, equipment detail, and service-history screens now give separate TAKE PHOTO and CAMERA ROLL / PHOTO LIBRARY choices.

AUTO UPDATE
- Cloud/API data remains no-store and is never service-worker cached.
- The app checks for updates at startup, when returning to the app, and periodically while open.
- Service-worker controller changes trigger one automatic reload so the new release becomes active without repeated manual refreshes.
- Registration uses updateViaCache:none when supported.

COMPATIBILITY
- Permanent GitHub Pages origin/path is unchanged.
- Existing ?unit=<id> and legacy ?equipment=<id> links are unchanged.
- Existing equipment/service history/Team accounts remain compatible.
