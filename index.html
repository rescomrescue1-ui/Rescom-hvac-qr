Shared Saving Setup

This version uploads both equipment records and service history to Airtable. If cloud sync fails, entries stay queued locally until Sync Now succeeds.

This app uses your Res-Com Airtable base as the shared filing cabinet. Enter a restricted Airtable Personal Access Token once on each device.

Recommended token access: only the “Res-Com Equipment Service Records” base, with data.records:read and data.records:write.

Use the normal Camera app on your phone or iPad.

How to open a Res-Com QR

1. Leave the Res-Com app.

2. Open the regular Camera app.

3. Point the camera at the Res-Com equipment QR label.

4. Tap the Res-Com website banner that appears.

5. The equipment record will open in the Res-Com app.

There is no longer an in-app QR scanner. This avoids the camera/scanner problems we were seeing across different devices.
