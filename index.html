Res-Com App Manual

New-user guide for the Res-Com HVAC QR Service History app.

Company Access — v30

Every Res-Com Admin and Tech now signs in with their own email and password. The first App Account created becomes Admin automatically.

New employee self-signups require the current 4-digit company PIN. The PIN is only for authorizing new employee account creation; it is not the employee login password.

Admins manage the Team list, roles, account status, temporary password resets, and the company PIN.

1. Getting Started

The Home screen is a simple QR dashboard with the Res-Com logo and fast actions to create labels, search a QR by ID/link, open Camera scan instructions, or report a problem. The Equipment tab contains the full searchable equipment manager.

First setup: connect the device with the restricted Res-Com Airtable token. If no App Account exists yet, the first person creates the first Admin account. After that, Admins and Techs sign in with their own email and password.

The app is responsive for phone, tablet, and desktop layouts and keeps the same GitHub Pages address so existing printed QR labels continue to work.

2. Company PIN

The 4-digit company PIN is now an Admin-managed employee signup code. It is required when a new Tech creates an account from the sign-in screen.

Admins can generate a new PIN from Team and prepare an email to the active Admins with the new code. Keep the PIN with Res-Com staff only.

The PIN is different from an employee password and different from the Airtable token.

3. What the Airtable Token Is

The Airtable token is a private digital key that allows the Res-Com app to read and write the company Airtable database over the internet.

Internet = connection. Wi-Fi or cellular data gives the phone internet access. Token = permission. The token proves the app is authorized to use the Res-Com records.

On a device that has never been connected, an Admin enters the restricted token once in the Connect This Device screen. The token stays on that device.

Do not put the token in GitHub source code, QR labels, customer emails, or public documents.

4. Online and Offline

When the phone is online and the token is valid, equipment and history sync with Airtable. Without internet, cloud records cannot refresh and photos cannot upload. Saved local equipment remains available as a fallback.

5. QR Workflow & Printing

Create Blank QR Label opens the label printer. Choose 1 label or enter a quantity up to 50, then generate the labels. Every generated label receives a different permanent RC code.

Print labels use a fixed, centered two-column Letter layout so each QR tag keeps the same width, height, QR size, and spacing.

The printed label shows QR CODE: RC-… directly underneath the QR so the equipment can still be searched if the camera cannot scan it.

Search QR by ID accepts either the printed RC code or the complete QR web link copied from Safari.

When the phone Camera opens a Res-Com QR in Safari, copy the QR ID or full QR link and paste it into Search QR by ID inside the app.

6. Creating New Equipment

  1. Tap Create Blank QR Label.
  2. Choose the quantity, generate the labels, then print and place one QR on each unit.
  3. Open the Unit ID through the QR workflow.
  4. Enter Customer / Site Name, Customer Email, Customer Phone, equipment type, manufacturer, model, serial, location, dates, notes, and photos.
  5. Save and confirm cloud sync.

Customer information now saves directly on the Equipment record; a separate customer Account is no longer required. Equipment Type can be selected from suggestions or typed manually.

7. Team Accounts

Tap TEAM from the bottom navigation to view the signed-in employee and the Res-Com employee account list.

The first App Account is Admin. New self-signups use the company PIN and start as Tech. Admins can create employees, switch Tech/Admin roles, disable or enable accounts, reset a temporary password, and generate the company PIN.

Each employee signs in with their own email and password. Service History saves the signed-in App Account email so entries are tied to the technician who performed the work.

8. Service History

Open an equipment record and add one of these entries: Service Call, Tune-Up / Preventive Maintenance, or Parts Replacement.

Enter the work notes, parts information when needed, and add service photos. The signed-in Res-Com App Account is used as the technician identity and email on new cloud history entries.

9. Warranty and Maintenance

Enter Warranty Expiration and Next Maintenance Due on each equipment record. Automated daily checks can email the Customer Email when either date is due.

10. Archive vs Delete

Archive hides retired/replaced equipment but keeps its history. Delete permanently removes the equipment and its linked history.

Use Archive for normal retired equipment.

11. App Updates & Appearance

Settings includes CHECK FOR APP UPDATE, System Health, Patch Notes, and WHITE / BLACK appearance choices. Strong contrast rules keep text readable in either appearance.

Keep the same GitHub Pages web address so existing printed QR labels continue to work.

Bottom Navigation

The main tabs are Home, Equipment, Team, and Bugs. The More menu contains Settings, Manual, What’s New, and System Health.

The layout is touch-friendly and responsive across phones, tablets, and desktop screens.

Loading Screen & Automatic Update Check

Every time the app starts, the Res-Com logo appears while the app checks version.json for a newer release before showing the normal app screen.

If a newer release is detected, the app refreshes cached files and reloads. If the phone is offline or the update server cannot be reached, the saved app still opens.

Bug & Recovery Center

Open BUGS from the swipeable bottom navigation or tap REPORT A PROBLEM on Home.

The app automatically records many JavaScript errors, failed Airtable requests, missing UI controls, and update-check problems with a unique bug/error code.

Use COPY BUG REPORT FOR CHATGPT and paste the copied report into ChatGPT. It includes the app version, device information, error codes, actions, and recent bug messages without including your Airtable token.

TRY QUICK REPAIR resets stuck buttons/navigation and refreshes cached app files. It does not erase equipment, the Airtable token, technicians, or the bug log. A Quick Repair cannot fix every programming bug, so use the copied report when the problem returns.

Bug reports save locally first. When Airtable is available, the app also attempts to save them to the shared App Bug Reports table.

Loading Screen Recovery — v29.2

The loading screen can no longer trap the app in an automatic update/reload loop. Version checks are informational and the app opens even if the update server is slow, unavailable, or temporarily out of sync.

A 2.5-second safety watchdog also removes any stuck loading overlay and returns the device to protected Technician Access instead of leaving a spinner.

For future updates, upload all patch files together. The app will show an update notice without repeatedly forcing a reload.

System Health

Open BUGS → SYSTEM HEALTH or Settings → System Health. The screen automatically runs the health check the first time it opens.

It checks the app server/update file, internet, Airtable connection, QR generator, Camera/QR workflow, local storage, service worker/cache, and critical navigation/buttons.

WORKING means the test passed. CHECK means the feature may need setup or is using a fallback. PROBLEM means the test failed.

Failed checks automatically create an HLT-… bug code in the Bug Log. Tap COPY HEALTH REPORT FOR CHATGPT to copy all test results plus the recent Bug Log.

System Health does not erase equipment or change Airtable data. Quick Repair remains a separate action that refreshes cached app files.

12. Troubleshooting

First step: open BUGS and copy the Bug Report for ChatGPT. If the app appears stale or a button is stuck, try Quick Repair.

No cloud history: check internet, verify the Airtable token, then refresh history.

QR opens Safari: copy the QR ID or QR link, open the app, then use Search QR by ID.

Photos will not upload: confirm internet and Airtable connection.

Technician names missing: refresh the app and make sure you are on the current version.

Old app version: use Check for App Update, close/reopen the Home Screen app, or re-add it from the same website URL.

Shared Saving Setup

This version uploads both equipment records and service history to Airtable. If cloud sync fails, entries stay queued locally until Sync Now succeeds.

This app uses your Res-Com Airtable base as the shared filing cabinet. Enter a restricted Airtable Personal Access Token once on each device.

Recommended token access: only the “Res-Com Equipment Service Records” base, with data.records:read and data.records:write.

Customer Email Reminders

Account email addresses are copied onto equipment records so Airtable can email customers when Warranty Expiration or Next Maintenance Due reaches today.

Reminder emails can run while the app is closed when the Res-Com reminder automation is enabled.

Device Token Setup

New device setup: paste the company Airtable token once, tap SAVE TOKEN TO THIS DEVICE, then VERIFY TOKEN ON THIS DEVICE. After that, the device remembers it.

Save and verify the Airtable token on this phone or iPad. Each device keeps its own saved copy.
