Res-Com App Manual

New-user guide for the Res-Com HVAC QR Service History app.

Company Login, Phone Number, Passwords & Face ID

When the app first opens, choose LOG IN or CREATE COMPANY ACCOUNT.

Each employee uses their company phone number as the login identifier. Auth0 stores the secure password and can verify the phone number by OTP when phone verification is enabled.

Create Company Account opens the Auth0 signup screen. Use the same company phone number that is saved in the Res-Com Company User profile.

Forgot Password is handled through Auth0 after entering the phone-number identifier.

When Auth0 passkeys are enabled, supported iPhones can use Face ID to approve passkey sign-in. Face ID biometric data stays on the iPhone.

The signed-in phone number is matched to the Airtable Company Users table for Technician Name and Admin/Technician role.

1. Getting Started

The Res-Com app tracks each piece of HVAC equipment by a permanent Unit ID and QR code. Equipment, service history, photos, customer accounts, warranty dates, and maintenance dates are shared through Airtable.

New device: open the Res-Com app, enter the company PIN, then open Settings and save the Airtable token to that device.

2. Company PIN

The 4-digit company PIN protects equipment records from casual access. The current default PIN is 2468. It can be changed in Settings.

The PIN is different from the Airtable token. The PIN unlocks the app. The Airtable token authorizes cloud access.

3. What the Airtable Token Is

The Airtable token is a private digital key. It allows the Res-Com app to read and write the company Airtable database over the internet.

Internet = connection. Wi-Fi or cellular data gives the phone internet access.

Token = permission. The token proves the app is authorized to use the Res-Com Airtable records.

To connect a new device: Settings → paste token → SAVE TOKEN TO THIS DEVICE → VERIFY TOKEN ON THIS DEVICE.

Do not put the token in GitHub source code, QR labels, customer emails, or public documents.

4. Online and Offline

When the phone is online and the token is valid, equipment and history sync with Airtable. Without internet, cloud records cannot refresh and photos cannot upload.

5. Opening Equipment From a QR

  1. Scan the equipment QR with the normal phone Camera app.
  2. Safari opens the Res-Com QR page.
  3. Tap COPY EQUIPMENT ID.
  4. Open the installed Res-Com Home Screen app.
  5. Tap PASTE EQUIPMENT ID.
  6. Enter the company PIN if requested.
  7. The equipment record opens.

iPhone Camera normally opens web QR links in Safari. That is why the copy/paste handoff is used.

6. Creating New Equipment

  1. Tap Create Blank QR Label.
  2. Print and place the QR on one unit.
  3. Open the Unit ID through the QR workflow.
  4. Enter account, equipment type, manufacturer, model, serial, location, dates, notes, and photos.
  5. Save and confirm cloud sync.

Equipment Type can be selected from suggestions or typed manually.

Accounts

Tap ACCOUNTS from the bottom navigation to create, search, and view customer/company accounts.

Tap any account card to open it. From the account screen you can edit Account Name, Email, Phone, and Notes.

Multiple equipment records can use the same customer account. Account Email is used for warranty and maintenance reminder emails.

8. Service History

Open an equipment record and add one of these entries: Service Call, Tune-Up / Preventive Maintenance, or Parts Replacement.

Select the technician, enter notes, parts information when needed, and add service photos.

9. Warranty and Maintenance

Enter Warranty Expiration and Next Maintenance Due on each equipment record. Automated daily checks can email the Account Email when either date is due.

10. Archive vs Delete

Archive hides retired/replaced equipment but keeps its history. Delete permanently removes the equipment and its linked history.

Use Archive for normal retired equipment.

11. App Updates

Settings includes CHECK FOR APP UPDATE and Patch Notes. Keep the same GitHub Pages web address so existing printed QR labels continue to work.

Bottom Navigation

The bottom bar is swipeable left/right in portrait mode. Tabs are Home, Equipment, Accounts, Settings, Manual, and Patch Notes.

Swipe across the bottom bar to reach any tab without rotating the phone.

12. Troubleshooting

No cloud history: check internet, verify the Airtable token, then refresh history.

QR opens Safari: copy the Equipment ID, open the app, tap Paste Equipment ID.

Photos will not upload: confirm internet and Airtable connection.

Technician names missing: refresh the app and make sure you are on the current version.

Old app version: use Check for App Update, close/reopen the Home Screen app, or re-add it from the same website URL.

Shared Saving Setup

This version uploads both equipment records and service history to Airtable. If cloud sync fails, entries stay queued locally until Sync Now succeeds.

This app uses your Res-Com Airtable base as the shared filing cabinet. Enter a restricted Airtable Personal Access Token once on each device.

Recommended token access: only the “Res-Com Equipment Service Records” base, with data.records:read and data.records:write.

Customer Email Reminders

Account email addresses are copied onto equipment records so Airtable can email customers when Warranty Expiration or Next Maintenance Due reaches today.

These reminder emails run from Airtable Automations, so they can send even while the app is closed.

Company Login Security

Individual passwords, password reset, and Face ID/passkeys are handled by Auth0. Employee passwords are not stored in Airtable.

The token is stored only in this device's browser storage. Do not put the token in the GitHub source files.

Company Users

Admins can add Res-Com employee profiles here. Passwords, password resets, and passkeys are managed by Auth0.

Technicians

Technicians saved here appear in the service-history dropdown on this device. Names found in downloaded cloud history are also added automatically.
